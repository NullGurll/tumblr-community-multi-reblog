# Privacy

Tumblr Community Multi-Reblog does not collect or transmit personal data to the
developer or to analytics services.

The extension:

- reads Tumblr's visible reblog composer and Community picker only to perform
  the actions requested by the user;
- stores batch progress and results locally in browser extension storage;
- does not request, read, or store the user's Tumblr password;
- does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, telemetry, or remote code;
- does not sell or share user data.

Removing the extension removes its extension-local stored batch information
according to the browser's normal extension-removal behavior.
